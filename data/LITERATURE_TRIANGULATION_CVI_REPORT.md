## Method (why this exists)

R6 of the 2026-10-19 SCI-readiness review required an Expert mini-Delphi CVI (5–8 TCM experts, 3–5 weeks). No expert pool is available. L-CVI is a defensible methodological **substitute**: instead of asking experts whether the framework items are relevant, we ask whether the underlying biological constructs are supported by multiple independent peer-reviewed sources.

**What L-CVI validates**

- Construct-level literature convergence: is the biology documented in ≥3 independent sources?
- Whether the framework is anchored in published mechanisms, not ad-hoc constructs.

**What L-CVI does NOT validate**

- Does NOT substitute for expert Delphi — no human expert judgement is captured.
- Does NOT validate framework-item acceptance — validates construct-level literature convergence only.
- Does NOT claim content validity — content validity requires expert panel.
- Textual_reference sources may be misattribution risks if the paper citation is wrong.

**Recommended submission posture**: Submit to IMR as a **commentary/framework paper** with L-CVI disclosed as a construct-level triangulation proxy. Explicitly flag that Expert Delphi is deferred to v2 as a stated limitation, not as a submission blocker.
