# Cover Letter — Journal of Integrative Medicine (JIM)

Dear Editor-in-Chief and Editorial Office of *Journal of Integrative Medicine*,

Please find enclosed our manuscript entitled:

**"An Integrated Steady-State Biology Framework for Non-Pharmacological Longevity Intervention: From Classical-Text × Genome Fusion to Closed-Loop Validation"**

submitted for your consideration as a **Hypothesis / Theoretical Framework** article.

**Why this manuscript fits JIM's scope**

JIM explicitly welcomes papers on methodology at the intersection of traditional medicine and modern science. Our work proposes the Integrated Steady-State Biology Framework (ISSBF), which operationalises classical Chinese-medicine constructs (qi-blood, zang-fu, yin-yang, zheng-xie) as eight measurable steady-state biology axes (autophagy/AMPK-mTOR, mitochondrial biogenesis/PGC-1α-SIRT1, senolytics, circadian CLOCK-BMAL1, neuroendocrine, inflammation, affect/HRV-EEG, epigenetic clock). It introduces a falsifiable gene-pathway × classical-candidate fusion rule and a Sense-Infer-Intervene-Verify (SIIV) closed loop, directly serving JIM's mission to advance integrative and evidence-based traditional medicine.

**What is new**

(1) A computable mapping from classical texts to eight quantifiable axes; (2) an intersection-based personalisation rule that forbids single-SNP decisions; (3) a 29-case real-world evidence base (L1=8 / L2=9 / L3=12) anchoring the framework to published human trials including the 2026 AFFIRM Phase II senolytics RCT; (4) an in-silico computational validation of the fusion engine (22 synthetic profiles) demonstrating 100% reproducibility, 100% coverage of weak-node profiles, 0 personalised output for control profiles, and 85% convergent validity against TCM domain expectations.

**Declarations**

- The work is original, has not been published elsewhere, and is not under consideration by another journal.
- **No new human-subjects research was conducted**: all human data are from cited published trials; the computational validation uses synthetic pathway scores only. Therefore IRB approval and informed consent are not applicable.
- No conflicts of interest are declared. The study received no external funding (self-supported independent project).
- A preprint has been (or will be) posted on medRxiv; the journal's preprint policy is respected.
- AI language models were used for manuscript editing and literature synthesis; all claims and data have been verified by the author against original sources.

We believe this framework provides a reusable, de-medicalised decision-support scaffold for non-pharmacological personalised intervention research, and fits JIM's readership well. Thank you for your consideration.
